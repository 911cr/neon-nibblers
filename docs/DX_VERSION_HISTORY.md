## Critical second pass

The current manufacturer's Deluxe collection is primarily a parts catalog. Its lack of a rules paragraph is not evidence that a mechanic is absent. The original product page and older manual are internally consistent about elimination rounds, whereas some modern rental/reseller pages combine that copy with CHOMPionship scoreboards. Those blended pages cannot establish DX's lifecycle. Likewise, reported prototype CPU difficulty and arena scaling are not released-DX proof.

No exact contemporary pellet/ghost/opponent/completion score values, match limit, join window, Find animation duration, map progression or tie policy were recovered from reliable DX documentation. Preserve configurable approximations and log them as unknown until a clean event sequence is measured.
